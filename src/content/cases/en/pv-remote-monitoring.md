## Background

The customer operated multiple distributed PV plants across regions; manual inspection was costly and fault detection lagged.

## Solution

- Deployed PV protocol converters and data acquisition terminals for inverters and meters
- Integrated with a PV O&M cloud platform for unified generation & revenue dashboards
- Automatic diagnostics with remote alarms on abnormal generation

## Results

- O&M labor cost reduced by ~40%; faster fault response
- Generation efficiency and revenue visualized for optimized operations

A PV power station needed centralized monitoring of its power-quality online monitoring devices and anti-islanding protection equipment. Data acquisition gateways collect harmonics, voltage, frequency and protection-event information and upload everything to the monitoring platform, helping O&M staff track power quality and protection status in real time.

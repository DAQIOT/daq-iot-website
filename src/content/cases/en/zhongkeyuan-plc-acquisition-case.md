A research institute of the Chinese Academy of Sciences operates multiple PLC-controlled experimental devices. IIoT acquisition gateways were deployed to read PLCs (Modbus TCP/RTU, S7, etc.) over Ethernet/serial links and upload operating data to the institute's internal data platform, enabling automatic recording, centralized monitoring and historical traceability of experimental data.

A steel plant's extensive steam pipeline network relied on manual inspection. Ultra-low-power NB-IOT wireless temperature/pressure sensors were installed at monitoring points, periodically reporting data to the cloud platform. Battery-powered and cable-free, they enable round-the-clock monitoring and abnormality alerts for the steam network.

**Cold-Chain Transport Vehicle GPS Positioning and Temperature-Humidity Management Case**

**1. Project Background**

Project name: Shanxi cold-chain transport vehicle GPS positioning and temperature-humidity management case.

Requirement: as economic development drives rapid growth in the logistics industry and transport scale expands, improving the logistics management system has become a top priority for the group. Temperature and humidity inside cold-chain transport vehicles and vehicle GPS latitude/longitude data needed to be reported to the logistics management system over MQTT in JSON format, enabling remote management and a clearer understanding of the transport process and vehicle interior conditions.

**2. Technical Solution**

Our GPS-positioning 4G wireless temperature-humidity sensor SC-GP-TH4G is used. The device has a built-in 4G IoT card and supports remote debugging, greatly simplifying on-site deployment and lowering construction cost. It supports UDP/TCP/MQTT/HTTP and other push methods to integrate data with the logistics management system.

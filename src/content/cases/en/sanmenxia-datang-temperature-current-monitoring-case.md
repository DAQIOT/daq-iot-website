The Sanmenxia Datang Huayang power plant required online monitoring of temperature and current inside distribution cabinets to prevent overheating failures. Wireless sensors collect cabinet temperature and loop current, upload data via wireless gateways, and trigger automatic alarms when limits are exceeded.

Substation distribution cabinets are prone to condensation during seasons with large temperature swings, creating creepage and short-circuit risks. LoRa wireless temperature/humidity sensors inside the cabinets feed a gateway that uploads data to the monitoring platform, which issues condensation risk warnings and can trigger heating/dehumidifying devices.

The CHN Energy Chifeng biomass power project required CEMS flue gas emission data to be reported to the grid authority platform via the IEC104 protocol. A protocol-conversion gateway collects emission data from the CEMS system and converts it into IEC104 messages for real-time upload over the power private network, achieving unattended automatic reporting.

## Background

The customer's workshop had 200+ heterogeneous devices (CNC, PLC, instruments) with fragmented data and no unified monitoring, making downtime troubleshooting slow.

## Solution

- Deployed DAQ-IOT wireless acquisition terminals and edge gateways compatible with Modbus / MQTT / OPC-UA
- Aggregated device data via a SCADA platform with real-time dashboards
- Configured multi-level alarm policies with automatic notifications

## Results

- Device connectivity reached 100%; downtime localization time reduced by ~60%
- Production OEE visualized for real-time management oversight

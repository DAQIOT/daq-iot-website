In the Zhongsheng Environment project, steam flow integrator data previously required manual meter reading. A data acquisition gateway now collects instantaneous/cumulative flow, temperature and pressure and uploads them to the cloud platform, enabling automatic meter reading, trend analysis and cost accounting.

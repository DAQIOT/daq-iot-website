## 1. Background

Distributed PV plants are mostly built on building rooftops and factory roofs. This makes later O&M inconvenient. PV data acquisition technology remotely collects data from auxiliary equipment and enables unattended operation. It reduces O&M costs, improves generation efficiency and delivers considerable economic value.

![PV Inverter Data Acquisition Solution](/images/plans/pv-inverter-data-acquisition/img-01.webp)

*PV Plant Data Acquisition Solution*

The inverter is the core component of a PV plant. It converts the DC power generated by PV modules into AC power for local loads or the grid (self-consumption or grid connection), and provides the associated protection functions. Its performance directly affects the efficiency and stability of the entire PV generation system, so remote data acquisition of inverters is highly valuable.

The inverter data that can be collected falls into two main categories: 1. PV module electrical parameters such as voltage, current, power, power factor and energy generation; 2. Fault and alarm information, including module and inverter faults and generation anomalies. Analyzing fault alarms and generation parameters improves efficiency and reduces losses.

## 2. Solution Implementation

2.1 Requirements Analysis: The following describes in detail the data acquisition for the inverters at a PV plant.

![PV Inverter Data Acquisition Solution](/images/plans/pv-inverter-data-acquisition/img-02.webp)

*Inverter Example*

Characteristics: the inverters are concentrated in a small area and of a single model, so an RS-485 bus is suitable. Compared with the high-cost option of giving each inverter its own acquisition stick, this offers two advantages: 1. Interface-based acquisition lets the user keep control of the data, unlike the manufacturer's acquisition sticks that are locked to a specific platform. 2. Lower cost: fewer hardware acquisition products, and compared with 4G wireless acquisition the corresponding data-traffic charges are also saved.

### 2.2 Acquisition Solution

![PV Inverter Data Acquisition Solution](/images/plans/pv-inverter-data-acquisition/img-03.webp)

*Inverter Data Acquisition Solution*

Implementation: 1. Connect the inverters to the Daq IoT gateway over an RS-485 line;

2. Configuration software pushes settings remotely to the cloud platform, which forwards them to the gateway over the MQTT protocol;

3. Collect inverter data over the Modbus RTU protocol and upload it to the cloud platform;

Voltage Violation and Reverse Overload Control in Distribution Transformer Areas

## Solution Background

C&I PV has grown rapidly under the dual-carbon (carbon peaking and carbon neutrality) goals, and the consequences now hold owners back: voltage violations and reverse overload in distribution transformer areas. Manual adjustment responds slowly and imprecisely, which is no longer enough for safe grid operation. How do you control a PV plant intelligently and precisely? The C&I PV smart control equipment from Shanghai Daq IoT Technology Co., Ltd. is built to answer that question.

## Product Solution

### System Principle

For distribution network problems such as voltage violations and reverse overload, the unit works with an AGC (automatic generation control) / AVC (automatic voltage control) terminal plus a power forecasting and control master station to form a closed loop — master station, terminal, executing device. Active and reactive power and voltage in the transformer area are then regulated precisely. The terminal acquires data locally, the transformer-area intelligent load forecasting system decides in real time, and commands execute on site; distributed generation, reactive compensation equipment and storage are coordinated so the system responds to grid abnormalities quickly. ### No Voltage Violations

PV output fluctuates widely, so the point of connection can exceed the voltage limit. The transformer-area intelligent load forecasting system monitors grid status continuously and, with an AI forecasting algorithm, adjusts inverters, SVG and other devices dynamically to keep voltage inside the safe band.

### Zero Reverse Overload

At midday, when PV output peaks, reverse power in the transformer area can overload the transformer. The system reads the load trend and either curtails output or absorbs the surplus with storage, so transformer-area equipment stays within its limits and lasts longer.

### Second-Level Response to Remote Commands

Over 4G/5G or fiber, site devices receive cloud control commands in real time and execute power and voltage adjustment in milliseconds. No manual intervention is needed, and O&M efficiency improves by more than 80%.

Distributed PV O&M is hard work as the PV industry grows. Take one renewable energy company whose 18 rooftop C&I distributed PV plants the Daq IoT team has served. The plants differ in scale, with output ranging from a few kilowatts to several megawatts, and their build dates span many years — the oldest went into service in 2012. The company had relied on manual inspection, and ran into four problems:

First, the plants are scattered and sited in difficult locations, so manual rounds are slow and carry safety risk;

Second, aging equipment fails often, and manual inspection cannot find and fix faults in time;

Third, data acquisition and management depend on manual work, so data goes missing and analysis is difficult;

Fourth, O&M cost is high, and the traditional model cannot keep up as the number of plants grows.

The customer needed IoT technology to make O&M intelligent, faster and cheaper. To work out how, the Daq IoT team surveyed a 1 MW rooftop plant on site. Built in 2016, it had 36 Huawei 33 kW inverters arranged in arrays; through combiner boxes and the grid-connection box, the array supplies local loads and exports surplus power to the State Grid.

The survey found that inverter data could only be read on site by hand, and only a limited set of values was available. Grid-connection energy data was transcribed by hand as well. That reading work alone cost the O&M company several thousand yuan a month. To solve it, Daq IoT developed an integrated hardware and software IoT solution for distributed PV plant O&M.

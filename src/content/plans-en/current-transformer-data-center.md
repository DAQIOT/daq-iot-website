Data centers have become the core of information storage and processing as information technology advances, and both their scale and their importance keep growing. Power O&M in turn is what keeps the whole power system stable and safe; a stable supply is the foundation every industry runs on. In that setting the DAQ-GP-CT485 current transformer, with its particular strengths, plays a critical role in data centers and in power O&M. The sections below set out where that importance shows.

## 1. Basic Principle and Features

The DAQ-GP-CT485 split-core current transformer from Shanghai Daq IoT Technology Co., Ltd. converts an AC analog current signal into an RS485 digital signal. It combines a 32-bit ARM MCU, a high-precision metering chip and a multi-point calibrated current conversion module, giving high accuracy and fast response. It exchanges data directly with common data acquisition gateways, DTUs, PLCs and host systems.

The product is widely used for current monitoring on AC motors, lighting, air compressors, heating, ventilation and air-conditioning equipment, building automation systems, equipment OEE and uptime tracking, industrial internet projects and rural grid upgrading.

## Role in the Data Center

In a data center, servers, switches and cooling equipment are the core assets, and they only run reliably on an accurate and appropriate current supply. The DAQ-GP-CT485 measures the current on each equipment circuit precisely. With real-time current data, O&M staff spot anomalies quickly. If the supply current to a server rises suddenly, for example, that may point to an internal fault or an overload. Staff can step in at the earliest stage with targeted action, prevent equipment damage and keep data center operations continuous.

![Current Transformers in Data Centers and Power O&M](/images/plans/current-transformer-data-center/img-03.webp)

*Role in power O&M*

In the power system, whether in generation, transmission or distribution, stable equipment operation depends on accurate current measurement. The DAQ-GP-CT485 is deployed at distribution cabinets in substations, at branch nodes on transmission lines and at customer terminals, transmitting current data in real time. When a short circuit, an overload or another fault sends current outside its normal band, O&M staff can find and locate the fault point quickly from that data, then isolate the circuit or repair the equipment in time. Faults stay contained, and supply across the system remains safe and reliable.

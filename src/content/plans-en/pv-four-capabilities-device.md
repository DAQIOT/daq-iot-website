## 1. Industry background: a mandatory requirement for distributed PV

As China's dual-carbon strategy advances, installed commercial and industrial distributed PV capacity is growing explosively. But once large volumes of distributed generation connect to the grid, grid operating safety, power quality and dispatch response become pressing issues.

Since 2023, provincial grid companies have issued policies one after another requiring distributed PV projects connected at 380 V / 10 kV to provide the "four capabilities" — observable, controllable, adjustable and measurable.

| Capability | What it means | Value to the grid |
|---|---|---|
| Observable | Real-time monitoring of PV plant status and data | Visibility of resource distribution across the network |
| Controllable | Ability to execute remote commands such as switching and power limiting | Supports grid frequency stability |
| Adjustable | Flexible or firm regulation following dispatch instructions | Improves power quality |
| Measurable | Accurate acquisition and forecasting of generation output and other key indicators | Maintains node voltage stability |

Without the four capabilities, grid connection is not permitted. With them, the question becomes how to be compliant, economical and reliable at the same time.

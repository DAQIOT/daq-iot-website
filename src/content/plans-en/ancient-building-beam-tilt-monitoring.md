## How Can Main-Beam Displacement in a Historic Building Be Warned Against Intelligently?

## IoT Monitoring Project at Qingming Riverside Landscape Garden, Kaifeng

### 1. Project background

Qingming Riverside Landscape Garden in Kaifeng is a national 5A-level scenic area and a national cultural industry demonstration base. Rebuilt 1:1 from the painting *Along the River During the Qingming Festival*, it is a flagship Song-culture tourism project in Central China. The viewing platform at the park's Dasong Parade Ground is a Song-style historic building that carries a heavy and constant flow of visitors, so the structural safety of its main beams and load-bearing columns directly determines visitor safety. To eliminate the risks caused by beam displacement, the park launched an online structural safety monitoring project.

![Smart Early Warning for Main-Beam Displacement in Historic Buildings (Qingming Riverside Landscape Garden, Kaifeng)](/images/plans/ancient-building-beam-tilt-monitoring/img-01.webp)

*2. Project pain points and requirements*

#### 1. Pain points

No cabling is allowed on a historic timber structure, so conventional monitoring methods are difficult to deploy.

Manual inspection is inefficient and the data always lags behind, making it impossible to know the structural state in real time.

Beam displacement is subtle and easily overlooked, so any delay in detection carries high risk.

#### 2. Core requirements

Real-time displacement monitoring at 8 key points on the main beam.

![Smart Early Warning for Main-Beam Displacement in Historic Buildings (Qingming Riverside Landscape Garden, Kaifeng)](/images/plans/ancient-building-beam-tilt-monitoring/img-02.webp)

*Cabling-free, battery-powered devices that are quick to install*

Remote data upload, visualization on a platform, and early warning.

A low-cost, highly reliable solution suitable for long-term operation in a scenic area.

### 3. IoT monitoring solution (highlights)

The project uses an integrated solution — 4G wireless laser distance measurement plus an IoT cloud platform — from Shanghai Daq IoT Technology Co., Ltd. to deliver intelligent structural safety monitoring.

![Smart Early Warning for Main-Beam Displacement in Historic Buildings (Qingming Riverside Landscape Garden, Kaifeng)](/images/plans/ancient-building-beam-tilt-monitoring/img-03.webp)

*1. Hardware and data acquisition*

Battery-powered laser distance meters, cabling-free and easy to deploy.

Installed at 8 monitoring points on the main beam, measuring the distance to ground reference points in real time.

Displacement of the main beam is determined precisely from changes in that distance.

#### 2. Wireless transmission

4G full-network transmission, free of on-site cabling constraints.

Stable data upload with low power consumption and uninterrupted operation.

#### 3. Platform visualization

Data is uploaded to the cloud in real time and can be reviewed remotely with trend-curve analysis.

Abnormal displacement triggers automatic alerts, enabling proactive safety management.

Historical data is fully traceable for maintenance and reporting.

![Smart Early Warning for Main-Beam Displacement in Historic Buildings (Qingming Riverside Landscape Garden, Kaifeng)](/images/plans/ancient-building-beam-tilt-monitoring/img-04.webp)

*4. Solution advantages*

Cabling-free, easy to install, low cost.

7×24 online monitoring with no staff on site.

Suited to complex sites such as historic buildings, scenic areas, exhibition halls and bridges.

Standardized IoT deployment that can be replicated quickly.

### 5. Project value and results

Improved safety: main-beam displacement is sensed in real time, so hidden risks are found and handled early.

More efficient management: remote, visualized supervision cuts the cost of manual inspection.

Fit for the site: solves the industry-wide problem that historic buildings cannot be cabled.

### 6. Summary

By combining IoT data acquisition, wireless transmission and cloud-platform alerting, this project puts the structural safety of the main beams at Qingming Riverside Landscape Garden under intelligent, digital management. It provides reliable technical support for safe park operations and stands as a proven reference case for structural monitoring in heritage and cultural tourism settings.

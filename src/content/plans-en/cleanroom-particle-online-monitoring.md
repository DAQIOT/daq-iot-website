### How Do Electronics and Pharmaceutical Plants Monitor Cleanroom Particles Accurately Online?

In chip manufacturing, pharmaceutical production and other industries with extreme environmental requirements, the airborne particle concentration in a cleanroom directly affects product yield and production safety. Traditional manual sampling is inefficient, the results lag behind and operator error can skew them. This article describes a high-accuracy, Ethernet-based cleanliness monitoring solution that needs no complex cabling and delivers 24-hour online particle acquisition in the cleanroom together with platform integration — a practical reference for controlling the production environment.

## 1. Core requirements and pain points

Electronics cleanrooms and pharmaceutical sterile workshops operate to strict cleanliness standards (ISO 14644-1, GMP and similar). Conventional management practice has several pain points:

Manual inspection is inefficient: staff carry a handheld particle counter from point to point on a schedule, which is time-consuming, cannot cover every hour of the day and leaves gaps in oversight;

Data is not timely: samples have to be sent to a laboratory for analysis, so results arrive late and a particle excursion is not caught in time;

Data does not connect: hand-recorded readings cannot feed directly into production management systems, so visual analysis and traceability are hard to achieve;

Monitoring points are constrained: wired devices require laborious cabling, some clean areas are difficult to work in, and adding points later is not flexible.

An online airborne particle counter addresses all of these points with automated, intelligent cleanliness control that matches the strict demands of the electronics and pharmaceutical industries.

*2. Core monitoring device and its advantages*

The solution uses a high-accuracy, Ethernet-enabled particle counter designed for cleanroom environments. It communicates over Ethernet and Modbus and connects reliably to an enterprise management platform. Its key advantages:

Multi-size particle measurement: it monitors particle concentration at 0.5 μm, 1.0 μm and other sizes simultaneously and displays particle counts, temperature, humidity, differential pressure and other key environmental parameters, with accuracy that meets industry standards;

Continuous online monitoring: 24-hour uninterrupted acquisition replaces manual inspection, giving full-time, full-coverage cleanliness monitoring;

Convenient communication and integration: the Ethernet interface with Modbus support connects directly to a workshop SCADA or MES system or a third-party data platform for automatic upload and centralized management;

Industrial-grade reliability: designed for cleanroom environments, with dust protection and immunity to interference, it runs stably over the long term and needs little maintenance;

Visual data display: the built-in high-resolution screen shows live data and historical curves, and indicator lights give a quick warning when values are out of range so on-site staff can respond immediately.

## 3. Implementation steps

#### 1. Planning and placing monitoring points

Decide the monitoring points from the cleanroom area and the production zones — key workstations, air returns, entry and exit points and so on — so that the critical controlled areas are covered;

Mount the devices on walls or brackets. No complex cabling is needed: a single Ethernet cable to the workshop LAN is enough, so deployment is flexible and the cleanroom envelope is not breached.

#### 2. Installation and commissioning

Fix the device, connect the network cable and power, and confirm it starts correctly;

Configure the IP address and communication parameters so the device is reachable from the enterprise management platform;

Calibrate the sampling flow rate and counting accuracy so the data meets the requirements of the relevant standard.

*3. Platform integration and data management*

Device data is integrated with the enterprise production management system over Modbus for centralized storage and visual display;

Alarm thresholds can be set for particle concentration, temperature, humidity and differential pressure, with automatic alerts notifying the responsible staff when values drift out of range;

The platform generates historical reports and trend curves and supports queries by time and by point, providing the data needed for environmental optimization and compliance audits.

## 4. Value and application scenarios

#### 1. Core value

Higher yield: real-time cleanliness monitoring catches particle excursions early and prevents product scrap caused by environmental contamination, protecting the quality of chips and medicines;

Lower management cost: manual inspection is replaced, and one manager can supervise several clean areas remotely, saving significant labor and time;

Compliance: complete historical records and traceability make GMP, ISO and other certification and regulatory inspections straightforward;

Better processes: data analysis identifies the causes of cleanliness fluctuation, so ventilation, personnel management and other steps can be optimized to raise efficiency.

#### 2. Where it applies

Electronics manufacturing: semiconductor and precision component production shops where fine dust must be kept away from the product;

Pharmaceutical production: aseptic preparation and API workshops that must meet GMP cleanliness requirements;

Laboratories and research institutes: precision instrument labs and biosafety labs where a stable environment is essential;

Other clean environments: food processing, optical lens production and any site with cleanliness requirements.

## 5. Routine maintenance and points to note

Regular calibration: calibrate flow rate and accuracy at the interval required by the relevant standard so the data stays accurate and reliable;

Remote inspection: check device online status and data on the management platform and catch offline devices and abnormal readings promptly;

On-site maintenance: clean the sampling inlet and housing regularly so dust does not block the inlet and affect accuracy;

Compliance records: keep monitoring data and calibration records properly to satisfy regulatory and audit requirements.

With this online cleanliness monitoring solution, electronics and pharmaceutical plants can manage cleanroom environments intelligently — protecting product quality and production safety while raising management efficiency. It is a practical choice for digital transformation in clean manufacturing.

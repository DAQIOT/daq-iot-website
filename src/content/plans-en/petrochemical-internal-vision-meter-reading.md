## How Do You Solve Meter Reading for Instruments Without a Communication Interface in a Petrochemical Workshop? An Intranet Vision Answer

In high-precision production environments such as petrochemical plants, mechanical analog gauges are widely used for their stability. They have no external communication interface, the site environment is complex, and manual reading is slow and lagged — a long-standing pain point in industrial data acquisition. The spinning line of the 1,500 t carbon fiber unit at Sinopec Shanghai Petrochemical Company Limited faced exactly this problem.

## 1. Core pain points: why traditional meter reading struggles

The project covers lines 1–4 of the spinning machines in the 1,500 t carbon fiber unit at Sinopec Shanghai Petrochemical. The workshop is fitted with a large number of mechanical pressure gauges. These instruments have no data output interface and cannot connect directly to an automation system. Corrosive gas is present on site, so standard equipment fails easily, and routing cables is difficult and carries high safety risk. Manual reading cannot deliver real-time monitoring, and the data cannot support line operation analysis in time.

To achieve real-time data acquisition and effective monitoring on the line, the project worked with Changjiang Institute to develop a custom intranet vision recognition wireless meter reading solution, using technology to break the traditional reading bottleneck.

## 2. Technical approach: vision, wireless and intranet for accurate reading

Given the structure of the pointer gauges and the corrosive environment, the project adopted a technical route of camera capture + wired transmission + vision recognition: the camera photographs the dial, the software recognizes the pointer reading, and the data is then read, stored and analyzed. One camera covers 1–5 gauges, which suits the dense installation on site.

![Intranet Vision Meter Reading for Instruments Without a Communication Interface in a Petrochemical Workshop](/images/plans/petrochemical-internal-vision-meter-reading/img-04.webp)

The hardware is the SC-GP-CAMpoe timed-capture camera (PoE version). Power and signal share one network cable, which greatly simplifies installation and reduces cabling hazards. For the corrosive environment, a custom protective enclosure keeps the device running reliably.

The system runs entirely on the intranet. Data never leaves the plant, so security and stability are both maintained.

## 4. Site implementation: handling corrosion and interference

Four cameras were deployed on site: two cover two gauges each and two cover three gauges each, with mounting optimized to match the gauge layout.

Commissioning focused on environmental interference: removing labels from the dials, preventing fogging inside the protective covers, adjusting mounting angles to eliminate light reflections, and cleaning the dials to keep recognition sharp — each factor affecting accuracy was eliminated one by one. Custom protective housings resist corrosion and keep the devices working stably over the long term.

*5. Results: what vision meter reading delivers*

The project achieved automated, intranet-based and visualized reading of mechanical analog gauges. The system recognizes instrument values precisely and stores them in the database automatically, matching each image path to its recognition result one to one, so the data can be queried, traced and analyzed.

The solution requires no modification to the instruments and no complex cabling. It suits corrosive, high-risk environments and resolves the data acquisition problem for instruments without a communication interface, offering a repeatable technical model for intelligent meter reading and digital upgrading of production lines in the petrochemical industry.

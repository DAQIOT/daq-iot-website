System overview

The Daq IoT PV O&M management platform combines data acquisition, data statistics, intelligent alerts and O&M management in one system. It helps PV plants raise generation efficiency, helps O&M companies improve efficiency and management while cutting O&M costs, and so reaches the overall goal of better economic returns. Smart gateways collect operating data and status from inverters, weather stations, integrated measurement and control devices and other equipment on site, enabling real-time online monitoring and diagnosis of the plant.

![Daq IoT PV Plant O&M Management Platform](/images/plans/pv-om-platform/img-01.webp)

*Application scenarios*

Monitoring and O&M management of commercial and industrial distributed PV plants

Monitoring and O&M of centralized PV sites

Residential PV and similar applications

Key functions

## 1. Online device monitoring

Inverters, combiner boxes, grid-connection equipment and weather stations are monitored in real time, with remote monitoring and remote meter reading. When a hidden fault is detected, O&M staff are notified promptly by SMS or phone call.

![Daq IoT PV Plant O&M Management Platform](/images/plans/pv-om-platform/img-02.webp)

*2. Online and offline O&M*

Complete O&M process management on cloud and mobile, with automatic flow of data and tasks, makes O&M standardized and efficient — raising O&M efficiency and lowering O&M cost.

![Daq IoT PV Plant O&M Management Platform](/images/plans/pv-om-platform/img-03.webp)

*3. Generation reports*

Built-in generation analysis reports across multiple dimensions; custom reports are also supported to meet the needs of different sites.

![Daq IoT PV Plant O&M Management Platform](/images/plans/pv-om-platform/img-04.webp)

*4. Energy flow diagram*

Presents the physical layout of equipment at every level of a PV site, together with the generation and loss information at each equipment node.

![Daq IoT PV Plant O&M Management Platform](/images/plans/pv-om-platform/img-05.webp)

*5. Inverter health assessment*

Comprehensive health analysis of inverters from equivalent utilization hours, power factor, conversion efficiency, string dispersion rate and other aspects.

![Daq IoT PV Plant O&M Management Platform](/images/plans/pv-om-platform/img-06.webp)

*6. Generation analysis*

Analysis of generation, generation efficiency, equivalent utilization hours and current (power) dispersion rate for a site or an inverter. Problems are found in time and generation efficiency improves.

![Daq IoT PV Plant O&M Management Platform](/images/plans/pv-om-platform/img-07.webp)

*7. PV data dashboards*

Multi-site PV dashboards and single-site dashboards are provided; the dashboard layout can also be defined to suit the situation.

System Overview

BRIEF INTRODUCTION

The energy management system is built on a modern service-oriented, cloud and mobile architecture. It acquires consumption data from every kind of energy monitoring point — power distribution, lighting, HVAC, water supply and drainage, gas and key equipment — and processes it into statistics by category, by sub-item, by time period and by area. Enterprises gain full visibility into energy use, see where consumption can be improved, optimize their energy use, cut overall consumption and reach their efficiency targets.

Business Background

BUSINESS BACKGROUND

As energy grows scarcer and the environment deteriorates, securing affordable, convenient and clean energy has become an urgent question for human survival and sustainable development. Finding ways to use energy more efficiently is now a shared responsibility, from individual households to enterprises and governments. Water, electricity and gas equipment and the associated consumption categories are a major part of industrial facilities and public infrastructure, and sensible layout of energy facilities plus effective control functions can markedly raise facility and energy efficiency while lowering cost. For many enterprises, rising energy bills are a real constraint on growth. Applying modern information technology to build a safe, stable, reliable, economical and efficient energy management system matters a great deal for cutting costs and improving competitiveness.

System Architecture — Local Deployment

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE-LOCAL DEPLOYMENT

![Integrated Water, Electricity, Gas and Heat Energy Monitoring Cloud Platform (Online Energy Monitoring and Efficiency Analysis)](/images/plans/integrated-energy-monitoring-cloud-platform/img-04.webp)

Application management layer

Built on a service-oriented and B/S architecture. It acquires, processes and stores energy data, and provides users with a range of web-based graphical data applications.

Network communication layer

Our DAQ communication management device converts fieldbuses to network connections on the LAN. It supports multiple serial ports and network ports and standards such as MODBUS, DL/T645 and MBus, and allows secondary development of communication protocols.

Field device layer

Fieldbus connections to the metering devices installed on site — electricity meters, water meters, flow meters and others — for real-time acquisition of consumption data.

Commercial Application Case

A Commercial Building in Qiantan, Shanghai

Project overview: a commercial and office complex of more than 200,000 square meters with many energy types and high consumption, where office tenants have stringent power quality requirements.

Owner requirements: on the basis of safe system operation, a detailed view of all consumption by category and sub-item, supporting continuous energy saving work and high-quality energy supply to tenants.

The energy management system offers several statistical modes — by category and sub-item, by area and by department — so users can see the building's energy use at a glance.

Web SCADA functions are used to design the power system diagram and monitor power system operation in real time to keep the system safe. Load and consumption alarm thresholds are set for each circuit, and out-of-limit events are notified to O&M staff by SMS and app.

The system provides meter reading, year-on-year, period-on-period and ranking analysis, so the owner always knows where consumption is abnormal and where improvement matters most.

The energy management system supports real-time power quality monitoring and can generate power quality reports periodically, providing the most direct data basis for improving power quality and raising electricity efficiency.

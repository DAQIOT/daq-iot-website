# ⚡ EDMI Mk6E Genius Smart Electricity Meter

## Advanced Smart Metering for Utility & Industrial Energy Management

The **EDMI Mk6E Genius Smart Electricity Meter** is a high-performance multifunction energy meter designed for utility companies, industrial facilities, smart grid systems, and commercial power monitoring projects.

It provides accurate electricity measurement, real-time energy monitoring, remote meter reading, and advanced communication capabilities for modern AMI (Advanced Metering Infrastructure) applications.

With support for RS485 communication, event logging, load profile recording, and anti-tamper protection, the EDMI Mk6E Genius is widely used in smart grid deployment, industrial automation, and utility energy management systems.

**1\. Product Introduction**

The Landis E850 series of high-end gateway settlement watt-hour meters are specially used for gateway settlement of regional power grids and power plants with high voltage levels, as well as large industrial and commercial users such as electrified railways with frequent load changes. With excellent measurement accuracy, significantly high refresh rate, and long-term stable and reliable operation, they have successfully won the unanimous praise and trust of users around the world.

# ❓ Landis+Gyr E650 Electricity Meter – FAQ (Utility & Smart Grid Applications)

* * *

### 1\. What is the Landis+Gyr E650 electricity meter used for?

The Landis+Gyr E650 is a high-precision smart electricity meter used for accurate energy measurement, billing, and monitoring in residential, commercial, and industrial power systems.

* * *

### 2\. Is the E650 suitable for smart grid and AMI systems?

Yes. The E650 is designed for **Smart Grid and AMI (Advanced Metering Infrastructure)** applications, enabling remote meter reading, load management, and grid optimization.

* * *

### 3\. What communication technologies does the E650 support?

It supports multiple communication options such as **PLC (Power Line Communication), RF, and wired/wireless interfaces** , depending on configuration and project requirements.

* * *

### 4\. Can the meter be remotely read and managed?

Yes. The E650 supports **remote data reading and management** , allowing utilities to collect consumption data and manage loads without on-site visits.

* * *

### 5\. Does the E650 record historical energy data?

Yes. It provides **detailed load profiles, historical consumption data, and event logs** , which are essential for energy analysis and billing systems.

* * *

### 6\. Is the Landis+Gyr E650 suitable for industrial applications?

Yes. It is widely used in **industrial facilities, commercial buildings, and utility networks** , offering reliable performance in demanding environments.

* * *

### 7\. Can it integrate with utility backend systems?

Yes. The E650 can be integrated with **MDM (Meter Data Management), SCADA systems, and utility billing platforms** for centralized energy management.

* * *

### 8\. What are the main advantages of using the E650 meter?

It offers **high accuracy, strong reliability, smart grid compatibility, remote communication capability, and long-term stability** , making it suitable for modern utility infrastructure.

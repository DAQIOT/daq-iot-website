# 1 **Product Overview**

DQ-GP-TL WiFi wireless three-color alarm light is a universal three-color alarm light based on WiFi wireless transmission and DC wide voltage power supply launched by Shanghai Shucai Internet of Things Technology Co., Ltd. It can monitor and indicate the working status of workshop equipment. It has the advantages of convenient and fast installation, no need to stop production, and truly lossless online installation. The color status of the three-color alarm light can be controlled through the http post API interface intranet, and it is widely used in CNC machine tools, electronic machinery, automated production lines and other industries when connected to the ERP MES system.

Multiple push methods are supported, including UDP/TCP/MQTT/HTTP. Users need to provide the public IP address and port number of the cloud server to receive data.

**Example of a JSON format data message:**

{" IMEI ": 860344048491162 , " Temperture ": 20 , " Humidity ": " 17.54 " , " DP ": " 12 " , "ICCID ": "1119205006447538" }

**Message parsing:**

{

" Temperture " : " 20.2 " // Temperature

" Humidity " : " 17.54 " // Humidity

" DP " : " 12 " // Dew point

"ID" : "860344048491162" // Device ID

}

# ❓ Dew Point Transmitter – FAQ (Industrial Applications)

* * *

### 1\. What is a dew point transmitter used for?

It is used to measure the dew point, humidity, and temperature of compressed air, industrial gases, and environmental systems to prevent condensation and ensure process quality.

* * *

### 2\. What communication protocols does the transmitter support?

Most dew point transmitters support **RS485 Modbus RTU** , allowing integration with PLCs, SCADA systems, and industrial IoT platforms.

* * *

### 3\. How accurate is the dew point measurement?

High-precision sensors provide **accurate readings with minimal drift** , ensuring reliable monitoring for industrial processes and compressed air systems.

* * *

### 4\. Can it operate in harsh industrial environments?

Yes. It is designed with **strong anti-interference and corrosion-resistant construction** , suitable for factories, compressed air networks, and manufacturing plants.

* * *

### 5\. How quickly does the sensor respond to changes in humidity?

The advanced sensing technology allows **fast response to moisture changes** , helping prevent condensation and maintain stable process conditions.

* * *

### 6\. Where is this transmitter commonly used?

It is widely used in **compressed air systems, HVAC systems, lithium battery production, pharmaceutical manufacturing, and industrial drying processes**.

* * *

### 7\. Can it provide continuous monitoring?

Yes. It supports **real-time continuous monitoring** for long-term industrial process control and energy-efficient operation.

* * *

### 8\. Is it easy to integrate into an existing system?

Yes. With **RS485 Modbus output and compact design** , it can be easily installed and integrated into SCADA, EMS, and other industrial monitoring platforms.

**❓ Explosion-Proof Cameras – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**

**1\. What is an explosion-proof camera?**

An explosion-proof camera is a specially designed industrial surveillance device that can operate safely in hazardous environments where flammable gases, vapors, or dust may be present. It is built with certified explosion-proof housing to prevent ignition sources from causing accidents.

**2\. What industries need explosion-proof cameras?**

Explosion-proof cameras are widely used in:

Oil & gas refineries

Chemical plants

Petrochemical facilities

Mining operations

Offshore platforms

Fuel storage and pipeline stations

Grain silos and dust-prone industrial areas

**3\. What certifications are required for explosion-proof cameras?**

Common certifications include:

ATEX (Europe)

IECEx (International standard)

UL / CSA (North America)

Ex d / Ex e / Ex t protection ratings

The exact certification depends on the installation region and hazard classification.

**4\. What does Ex d mean in explosion-proof classification?**

“Ex d” refers to flameproof enclosure protection. It means the camera housing can withstand an internal explosion without allowing it to spread to the surrounding explosive atmosphere.

**5\. Can explosion-proof cameras be used outdoors?**

Yes. Most explosion-proof cameras are designed with IP66/IP67 or higher protection rating, making them suitable for outdoor harsh environments including rain, dust, humidity, and extreme temperatures.

**6\. What video quality do explosion-proof cameras support?**

Depending on the model, explosion-proof cameras typically support:

1080P Full HD

2MP / 4MP / 5MP / 4K options

Infrared night vision for 24/7 monitoring

**7\. Are explosion-proof cameras compatible with IP or PoE systems?**

Yes. Most modern explosion-proof cameras support IP network connectivity and PoE (Power over Ethernet) for easy installation and integration with existing surveillance systems or VMS platforms.

**8\. Can explosion-proof cameras work in gas or dust environments?**

Yes. They are specifically designed for hazardous gas zones (Zone 1 / Zone 2) and dust zones (Zone 21 / Zone 22) depending on certification level, ensuring safe operation in explosive atmospheres.

**9\. How are explosion-proof cameras installed?**

Installation methods usually include:

Wall mounting

Pole mounting

Ceiling mounting (depending on enclosure design)

All installations must follow certified explosion-proof wiring and sealing requirements to maintain safety compliance.

**10\. Do explosion-proof cameras require regular maintenance?**

Yes, but maintenance is minimal. Regular inspection is recommended to ensure:

Housing integrity is intact

Seals and cable glands are properly secured

Lens is clean and unobstructed

Network connection is stable

Proper maintenance ensures long-term safe operation in hazardous environments.

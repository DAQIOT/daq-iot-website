**Product Details**

SCADA Graphical Configuration Gateway is a gateway product for SCADA systems. It supports graphical configuration, collects data from field devices, and connects on-site equipment to SCADA and cloud platforms, making centralized monitoring and management possible in power, water, energy and industrial automation projects.

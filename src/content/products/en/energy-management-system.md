**Product Details**

**System Brief Introduction**

The Energy Management System is built on the latest service-oriented, cloud-computing and mobile architecture. By collecting energy-consumption data from various monitoring points (substation and distribution, lighting, air conditioning, water supply and drainage, gas, key equipment), it performs professional data analysis and processing to produce statistical analysis of energy consumption by category, item, time and area. This gives enterprises full visibility into energy use, intuitively reveals optimization opportunities, and helps them optimize energy use, reduce overall energy consumption and achieve energy-saving and efficiency gains.

![](/images/products/1741835785212816.png)

**Major Functions**

| Function | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Energy data acquisition | Supports mainstream communication protocols and various communication interfaces, wired or wireless |
| Energy data statistics | Automatic hourly/daily/monthly/yearly, year-on-year, month-on-month and share analysis; classification/item/area/group analysis; energy-use ranking |
| Line-loss analysis | Timely detection of leakage and waste |
| Energy alarm | Configurable safe-operation and energy-use alarm thresholds with sound-and-light, SMS and App alarms |

**Business Background**

As energy becomes scarcer and the environment deteriorates, obtaining economical, convenient and environmentally friendly energy has become an urgent issue for human survival and sustainable development. Finding ways to improve energy efficiency has become a shared responsibility of society — from households to enterprises and governments. Water, electricity and gas equipment and classified energy consumption form a major part of industrial facilities and social infrastructure; a sensible layout of energy facilities and control functions can significantly improve facility and energy efficiency and reduce costs. For enterprises, rising energy costs are a real development challenge. Building a safe, stable, reliable, economical and efficient energy management system with high-tech information technology is of great significance for reducing enterprise costs and improving product market competitiveness.

**Application Scenarios**

The Energy Management System can be widely used in industrial enterprises, commercial buildings, public buildings and other energy-consuming sites, helping owners fully understand energy-use information, identify unreasonable energy use, and receive energy-saving optimization suggestions, ultimately achieving safe, economical and green energy use.

**System Architecture — Local (Private) Deployment**

![](/images/products/1741932646132270.png)

**Application management layer**

A service-oriented and B/S architecture. It collects, processes and stores energy-consumption data and provides users with various web-based graphical data applications.

**Network communication layer**

Through our DAQ communication management unit, various field buses are converted into network access to the LAN. It supports multiple serial and network ports and standard protocols such as MODBUS, DL/T645 and MBus, with secondary development of communication protocols supported.

**Field device layer**

Field buses connect various metering devices installed on site, such as electricity meters, water meters and flow meters, to acquire and monitor energy-consumption data in real time.

**Application case — an industrial park in Hebei**

Project overview: the park has more than 40 unattended box-type substations and hundreds each of water meters, gas meters and heat meters; management and O&M face huge challenges.

Owner requirements:
1. Ensure electrical safety through the energy management system
2. Effectively manage all types of energy use in the park and continuously reduce energy costs
3. Improve O&M and management efficiency through a cloud platform

![](/images/products/1741837495141416.png)

Implementation: all smart devices are connected to the cloud platform over field buses plus a fiber-optic network. Managers can monitor and manage on the move via a browser or App. The platform monitors thousands of metering devices in total, with more than 400,000 data points.

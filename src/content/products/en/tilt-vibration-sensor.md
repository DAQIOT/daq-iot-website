**Product Details**

# 1 Product Overview

![](/images/products/1780995595698044.png)

DAQ-LP-TVNB tilt & vibration sensor is an intelligent monitoring device launched by Shanghai DAQ IoT Technology Co., Ltd., specially designed for bridges, viaducts, roads and buildings. It adopts NB-IoT technology and integrates core functions such as vibration monitoring and online warning, enabling real-time monitoring of safety status and timely detection of abnormal events such as damage and collision with alarms triggered, providing round-the-clock, all-dimensional intelligent protection for infrastructure safety.

It is suitable for all displacement measurement needs of bridges, viaducts, roads and buildings, and can be widely used in the safety monitoring of infrastructure such as municipal transportation, expressways and bridges/tunnels/buildings.

# 3 Core Advantages

Extreme environmental adaptability: supports wide-temperature operation from -40℃ to +80℃, with IP67 high protection rating, adapting to various harsh weather conditions;

Low power consumption and long endurance: powered by a 6000mAh lithium-thionyl chloride battery, with an annual self-discharge rate of ≤2% and a design service life of ≥3 years;

Intelligent and precise monitoring: false alarm rate below 1%, with adaptive learning that optimizes the monitoring algorithm according to the actual scenario;

Fast alarm response: abnormal events are reported in real time, and it supports linkage with information boards and public address systems for intuitive warning effects.

# 5 Core Functions

## 5.1 Online Monitoring

The device supports 24-hour online monitoring and actively uploads monitoring data via the NB-IoT network. Users can view device working status, battery level, monitoring data and other information in real time on the backend. The heartbeat reporting frequency is independently configurable (adjustable from 5-9999 minutes) to meet the monitoring needs of different scenarios.

## 5.2 Alarm Function

It has special vibration monitoring capability. When abnormal vibration such as guardrail damage or collision is detected, the device automatically wakes up and triggers an alarm. The alarm signal is uploaded to the management platform in real time via the NB-IoT network, ensuring staff obtain abnormal information at the earliest opportunity.

## 5.3 Warning Function

During an abnormal alarm, the device's built-in warning light flashes; it can also be linked with optional information boards and public address systems for both visual and audible warning, effectively deterring destructive behavior. The backend supports configuring the warning light flashing within an authorized time period, improving warning flexibility.

**Product Details**

# 1 Product Overview

![](/images/products/1692863089159158.png)

SC-GP-EM4G wireless energy monitoring meter is an intelligent power parameter monitoring device launched by Shanghai DAQ IoT Technology Co., Ltd., supporting transmission modes such as 4G/WiFi/LoRa. It can monitor the line voltage and current of single-phase and three-phase equipment, and can calculate active power, apparent power, power factor, operating frequency and active energy. It monitors the operating and power consumption conditions of single-phase and three-phase electrical equipment in real time, ensuring the safety of electrical equipment and enabling unattended monitoring. When combined with relay control devices, it can automatically cut off the power supply in case of overvoltage or overcurrent. It is widely used in scenarios such as environmental protection power monitoring and monitoring of factory equipment working status.

## 3.1 Hardware Parameters

Housing surface treatment: ABS soft-touch oil

Weight: 200g

Installation: wall-mounted

Operating environment: -20°C ~ 70°C, 0 ~ 95% RH without condensation

## 3.2 Functional Characteristics

Power supply: DC 8~12V (terminal), with reverse and misconnection protection

Power consumption: 0.24W

Number of channels: 6 channels of AC current and voltage

Current transformer: 2000:1 (default)

Sampling interval: 1s / channel

Voltage measurement range: 100V-400V

Voltage measurement accuracy: 0.5% rdg.

Current measurement range: 0-50A

Current measurement accuracy: 2% rdg.

Communication interface: RS-485, RS-485 (2-wire) dual-port parallel connection, with surge and electrostatic (4000V) protection on the RS-485 line

Parameters: 115200bps 8,N,1

Maximum communication distance: 1.2 km

Communication protocol: Modbus RTU

Response speed: ≤100ms

Device address range: 1~255

**Product Details**

# 1 Product Overview

![](/images/products/1780553031135433.png)

HD serial camera

DAQ-GP-SPCAM is a serial camera launched by Shanghai DAQ IoT Technology Co., Ltd. It can be widely used in mobile scenarios such as dynamic water conservancy monitoring, power monitoring, transportation industry monitoring, video doorbell remote monitoring, video phones and video attendance devices. It is especially suitable for narrowband image transmission, such as remote image monitoring systems based on telephone lines, GPRS, data radio and other low-speed channels.

# 3 Product Features & Parameters

Outputs complete JPEG files;

Images available in multiple resolutions: 160×128, 320×240, 640×480, 1280×720, 800×600, 1280×1024, 1280×960, 1920×1080, etc.;

RS232/RS485 level serial port;

Multiple adjustable transmission rates; the serial interface supports 9600, 19200, 38400, 57600 and 115200 baud rates;

Optimized serial communication protocol, supporting passive transmission so the host can be configured with a smaller cache; passive mode supported, the camera can first cache one image, any packet of image data can be requested, and the packet size can be set.

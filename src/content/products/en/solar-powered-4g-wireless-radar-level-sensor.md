**Product Details**

# 1 Product Overview

![](/images/products/1783412748151745.png)

![](/images/products/1783412768150837.png)

DAQ-SP-RL4G solar-powered 4G wireless radar level sensor is a wireless liquid level measurement product launched by Shanghai DAQ IoT Technology Co., Ltd. It integrates a 79~81GHz millimeter-wave radar level sensing unit, a solar power module, a lithium battery energy storage unit and a 4G wireless transmission module. It continues the core advantages of radar sensors — high accuracy and high stability — while being equipped with a low-power control circuit, requiring no external mains power and relying on solar power.

The terminal has a built-in rechargeable lithium battery and is rainproof for outdoor use, making outdoor installation convenient. The built-in 4G IoT SIM card allows the periodically collected data to be connected directly to the IoT cloud platform, and real-time device data can be viewed directly on a mobile app or browser. It is suitable for various remote monitoring scenarios where outdoor power supply is inconvenient.

# 1 Product Overview

![](/images/products/1781161021547006.png)

![](/images/products/1781161181316944.png)

![](/images/products/1781161313167950.png)

The MV-CS050-L16 5-megapixel high-definition color camera is a 4G or Wi-Fi wireless camera for timed photography, snapshot video recording and FTP photo upload. It has a built-in white-light source and is widely used for capturing and recognizing readings on instruments and meters, as well as for photo capture and video monitoring in cultural-relic, geotechnical, hydrological and water-conservancy applications.

The product connects to the network over Wi-Fi, 4G or Ethernet, takes photos on a schedule, and uploads them to a designated FTP server or stores them on an SD card. With a 4G wireless module it can be used outdoors where no Wi-Fi network is available.

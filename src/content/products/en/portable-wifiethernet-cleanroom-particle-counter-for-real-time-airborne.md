# 1 **Product overview******

DAQ-GP-CL cleanliness monitor terminal is a multi-functional dust particle counting device based on wireless transmission launched by Shanghai Digital Mining Internet of Things Technology Co., LTD. It takes air samples through the built-in air pump, and then the number of particles of different sizes is calculated by the built-in microprocessor. It can collect the dust particle number, temperature and humidity, dew point, wind direction and wind speed data in the environment.

The cleanliness monitor supports Lora/Lorawan/ WIFI / RJ 45 network port communication mode, easy operation, intuitive interface analysis, built-in lithium battery, equipped with standard Type-C charging port, easy to carry. It is suitable for the environmental air quality testing of blood center, CDC, quality supervision institute and other authoritative institutions, as well as pharmaceutical workshop, semiconductor workshop and other production enterprises and scientific research departments.

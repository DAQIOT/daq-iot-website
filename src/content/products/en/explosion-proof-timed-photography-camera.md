# 1 Product Overview

SC-GP-CAM is a camera launched by Shanghai DAQ IoT Technology Co., Ltd. that supports timed photography / snapshot video recording and FTP photo upload. It can be widely used for reading and recognizing instrument displays, for photo capture and video monitoring in the fields of cultural relics, geotechnical engineering, hydrology and water conservancy.

# 1 Product Overview
DAQ-GP-VCMT5G all- in-one 5G fusion terminal is a control terminal launched by Shanghai Shucai IoT Technology Co., Ltd. that integrates 5G communication, vertical encryption, telecontrol communication, and AGC/AVC functions .

## 4.7 Protocol Library List
The all-in-one 5G converged terminal runs the LPC data acquisition core software platform ( LPC software for short ). Each communication protocol is an independent SO library in the LPC software . ## FAQ – 5G Industrial Telecontrol Gateway

### 1\. What is a 5G Industrial Telecontrol Gateway?

A 5G Industrial Telecontrol Gateway is a device that enables real-time remote monitoring and control of industrial equipment through 5G networks. It acts as a secure bridge between field devices (PLC, sensors, RTU) and cloud or control centers.

* * *

### 2\. What is AGC/AVC in this gateway?

AGC (Automatic Gain Control) and AVC (Automatic Voltage Control) help stabilize signal quality and power levels. This ensures reliable communication even in environments with weak or fluctuating signals.

* * *

### 3\. How secure is the IoT communication?

The gateway uses end-to-end encryption and industrial-grade security protocols to protect data transmission. It prevents unauthorized access and ensures secure remote control of critical infrastructure.

* * *

### 4\. Can it work with existing SCADA or PLC systems?

Yes. The gateway supports standard industrial protocols such as Modbus, MQTT, and TCP/IP, allowing seamless integration with most SCADA systems, PLCs, and industrial IoT platforms.

* * *

### 5\. What industries can use this telecontrol gateway?

It is widely used in:

  * Smart grid and energy systems

  * Water treatment and pumping stations

  * Oil & gas remote monitoring

  * Industrial automation

  * Smart factories and infrastructure monitoring

* * *

### 6\. Does it support real-time remote control?

Yes. With 5G ultra-low latency, the gateway enables real-time data transmission and remote control of industrial equipment with minimal delay.

* * *

### 7\. Can it operate in harsh environments?

Yes. The device is designed with industrial-grade protection, including wide temperature range, vibration resistance, and IP-rated enclosure for outdoor or harsh industrial conditions.

* * *

### 8\. Is remote configuration and maintenance possible?

Yes. The gateway supports remote configuration, OTA (over-the-air) updates, and diagnostics, reducing on-site maintenance costs.

* * *

### 9\. What is the installation process like?

Installation is simple: connect the field devices, insert the SIM card, configure network parameters, and integrate with your cloud or control platform. Technical support is available if needed.

* * *

### 10\. Does it support dual communication or backup links?

Yes, optional configurations can support dual SIM or backup network links to ensure continuous connectivity in mission-critical applications.

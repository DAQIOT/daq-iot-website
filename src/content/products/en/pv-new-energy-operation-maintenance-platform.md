**Product Details**

PV New Energy Operation & Maintenance Management Platform is a software platform for the operation and maintenance management of photovoltaic power plants. It provides centralized monitoring of generation data, device status, alarms and reports, helping operators improve plant efficiency and reduce O&M costs.

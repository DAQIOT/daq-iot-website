**Product Details**

![](/images/products/1739848673186797.png)

We provide nationwide construction, installation, operation and commissioning services. Typical scopes include the installation, repair and on-site commissioning of matching sensors and gateway monitoring devices. Our services also cover structured cabling systems, video surveillance systems, computer networks, multimedia systems, remote video conferencing systems, large-screen display systems, public address systems, access-control (one-card) systems and other weak-current systems.

We maintain long-term close partnerships with O&M service providers such as the Weak-Current O&M Alliance, Lenovo's Sunyard and IT Gas Station. Outside remote areas, local installers can be dispatched within 100 km, which greatly improves service timeliness and significantly reduces the travel cost of non-technical personnel.

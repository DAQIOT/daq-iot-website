**Product Details**

DCS Environmental Protection Monitoring & Management System is a distributed control system (DCS) based platform for environmental protection monitoring and management. It collects data from environmental monitoring instruments and production equipment and provides real-time monitoring, alarm management and reporting for industrial plants.

**Overall Data Acquisition Solution for PV Power Station Project Monitoring & O&M**

A grid-connected PV power generation system is a system in which the DC electricity generated by solar cells from solar radiation energy (i.e., the "photovoltaic effect" of solar cells) is converted into AC electricity by a grid-connected PV inverter and fed into the grid; in essence, it is a power station that generates electricity using solar energy. Compared with power stations that generate electricity from traditional energy sources such as coal, oil and natural gas, grid-connected PV power generation systems have the advantages of being renewable and pollution-free.

The PV modules generate current through the photovoltaic effect, which is then inverted by the inverter, converting DC into AC. It is then stepped up by the PV transformer to 10kV and connected to the 10kV busbar of the grid-connection cabinet in the electrical room.

The electricity meter protocol supports DLT645-1997/2007 and DLT698.45 protocols.

The acquisition gateway supports smart surge protection combiner boxes, DC surge protection distribution cabinets, inverter systems, AC surge protection distribution cabinets, transformers, system distribution and box transformers, etc. Leveraging rich experience and resources in electrical system solution integration and supply chain integration, the product supports the power E-file standard and the IEC61850 protocol, is compatible with inverter components, lateral partition devices and high/low-voltage distribution cabinets from various manufacturers, and can integrate with various power monitoring systems and power dispatch systems, enabling rapid implementation of PV power station projects.

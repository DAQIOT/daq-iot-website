The Mk30 is a compact, single-phase electrical meter designed for accurate energy and power monitoring in residential, commercial, and industrial environments. Its DIN rail mounting ensures easy installation, and it supports multiple measurement parameters including active/reactive energy, voltage, current, and power factor.

## Key Features

  * **High Accuracy Measurement:** Class 1 for active energy and Class 2 for reactive energy.

  * **Multi-Parameter Monitoring:** Active/Reactive/Apparent Power, Voltage, Current, Power Factor.

  * **DIN Rail Mounting:** Compact 35mm rail design for easy installation.

  * **Optional Communication:** RS485 Modbus RTU for smart grid and remote monitoring.

  * **Pulse Output:** Optional S0 standard for external data acquisition.

  * **Reliable & Durable:** Designed for harsh industrial and commercial environments.

## FAQ

**1\. What is the accuracy class of Mk30?**  
Active energy: Class 1, Reactive energy: Class 2 according to IEC standards.

**2\. Can Mk30 be integrated into SCADA or IoT systems?**  
Yes, optional RS485 Modbus RTU communication allows seamless integration.

**3\. Is this meter suitable for harsh environments?**  
Yes, designed for -25°C to +55°C and IP20 protection.

**4\. What is the mounting type?**  
DIN rail 35mm standard mounting for easy installation.

**5\. Does Mk30 support pulse output?**  
Yes, it has optional S0 pulse output for external data acquisition.

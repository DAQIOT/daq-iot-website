## 4.5 **Communication features**

- Equipped with 2 RS485 ports, used to connect to the RS485 port of the energy meter or the photovoltaic inverter interface

- With low voltage power line carrier, used to forward the communication commands of the concentrator

- With Bluetooth communication for on-site maintenance

## 4.6 ******Communication networking****features**

The network is formed by carrier or dual-mode . Depending on the carrier or dual-mode technology adopted, two working modes are supported: working with address and working without address.

## 5.3 **Installation and wiring precautions**

Common problems with power cord wiring:

The converter power cable and power supply have poor contact; the converter power cable should not be connected to 380V power; the power cable must not be connected to the RS-485 signal cable.

Common problems with RS-485 line wiring:

The converter RS-485 line and the meter RS-485 line are connected in reverse; the converter RS-485 line A and line B are short-circuited;

The 485 bus is short-circuited ; the RS-485 line of the interface of some electric meters or photovoltaic inverters is not connected to the 485 bus; the 485 buses under more than two converters are connected together.
